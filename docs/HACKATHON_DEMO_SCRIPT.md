# TripRescue — Hackathon Demo Script

## Target Duration: 3–5 minutes

## Core Story
**Trip breaks → TripRescue understands → TripRescue rebuilds → Trip continues.**

---

## Script

### 1. Opening (15 seconds)

> "Meet TripRescue — when your trip breaks, we rebuild it."

> "Today I'm going to show you what happens when a real trip falls apart, and how TripRescue not only understands the problem, but fixes it."

---

### 2. The Trip — "Manali Adventure" (30 seconds)

Show the **Trip Dashboard** (Screen 04).

> "Here's a real trip: Manali Adventure. Pune to Delhi to Chandigarh to Manali."

Point to the connected itinerary timeline:

> "Notice this isn't just a list of bookings — it's a **connected system**. Flight connects to airport transfer, connects to hotel, connects to train, connects to local transfer, connects to the Rohtang Pass activity."

Point to Trip Health:

> "Trip Health: 92%. Everything is confirmed and connected. Life is good."

**Key visual:** The connected timeline with green/safe indicators and dependency lines.

---

### 3. Disruption Hits (20 seconds)

Click **"Simulate Disruption"** → Navigate to Disruption Center (Screen 05).

> "Now — the Pune to Delhi flight is delayed by 5 hours."

Select "Flight delayed", choose the Pune → Delhi flight, enter 5 hours.

> "That's it. Just one delayed flight. Let's see what TripRescue discovers."

Click **"Analyze Impact"**.

---

### 4. The Wow Moment — Impact Analysis (45 seconds)

Impact Analysis screen loads (Screen 06).

> "Here's where TripRescue is different from anything else."

**Pause for 3 seconds. Let the dependency graph visual speak.**

Point to the ripple:

> "One flight delay — and look what happens. The airport transfer? **Missed.** The hotel check-in? **Delayed.** The train to Chandigarh? **The train departs before the flight even lands.** The Manali transfer? **Broken.** The Rohtang activity? **At risk.**"

Point to Impact Summary:

> "3 bookings are critical. 2 more are at risk. 5 out of 6 bookings affected by a single delay."

> "**This** is the problem no one solves today. Your airline app doesn't know about your train. Your hotel app doesn't know about your flight. TripRescue sees the entire picture."

---

### 5. Recovery Plans (30 seconds)

Click **"Generate Recovery Plans"** → Recovery Plans screen (Screen 07).

> "In seconds, TripRescue generates three recovery options."

Point to the three cards:

> "**Best Overall**: ₹2,100 additional, just 1.2 extra hours, keeps your hotel and your Rohtang activity — 92% of your trip preserved."

> "**Cheapest**: Only ₹900, but you lose 3.5 hours, lose your hotel night, and the activity is at risk."

> "**Fastest**: ₹4,500 — zero extra time, 100% preserved — but costs the most."

---

### 6. Why This Plan? (20 seconds)

Point to recommendation explanation:

> "TripRescue explains **why** Best Overall is recommended: it costs ₹1,200 more than the cheapest but saves 2.3 hours and preserves your hotel and your important activity."

> "This is **explainable intelligence** — not just 'AI says this is best.'"

---

### 7. Compare & Decide (15 seconds)

Optionally show Plan Comparison screen (Screen 08):

> "You can compare every dimension side by side — cost, time, preservation, convenience — and make an informed decision."

---

### 8. Apply Recovery (20 seconds)

Click **"Choose Best Overall"** → Recovery Confirmation (Screen 09).

> "Before we apply anything, TripRescue shows you exactly what changes and what stays the same."

Point to Before → After:

> "Your train is rebooked to 9:00 PM, your transfer is updated. Your hotel and your Rohtang activity? Preserved. Refund of ₹500 from the old bookings, ₹2,600 for new ones, net cost ₹2,100."

Click **"Confirm & Apply Recovery"**.

---

### 9. Trip Back on Track (20 seconds)

Updated Trip screen loads (Screen 10).

> "**Your trip is back on track.**"

Point to Trip Health transformation:

> "Trip Health went from 92% down to critical during the disruption — and now it's back to **98%**."

Point to the recovered timeline:

> "Two bookings changed, four preserved, ₹2,100, 1.2 additional hours. Trip Stable."

**Pause. Let this sink in.**

---

### 10. Closing (15 seconds)

> "One delayed flight. Five affected bookings. Three recovery plans. One confirmation. Trip saved."

> "**When your trip breaks, TripRescue rebuilds it.**"

> "Thank you."

---

## Key Demo Tips

| Tip | Why |
|-----|-----|
| **Pause on the Impact Analysis graph** | This is your differentiator — let judges absorb the ripple effect |
| **Say "connected system" not "dependency graph"** | Judges understand systems, not graph theory |
| **Emphasize "explainable"** | Every recommendation has a traceable reason |
| **Don't rush the before/after** | The transformation is the payoff |
| **End on "Trip Stable"** | Rewarding conclusion the audience remembers |
